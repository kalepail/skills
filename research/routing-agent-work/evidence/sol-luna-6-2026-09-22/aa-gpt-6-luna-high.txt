Comparison Summary
GPT-6 Luna (high) is amongst the leading models in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.
GPT-6 Luna (high) scores 32 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 12). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 47M tokens, which is fairly concise in comparison to the median of 85M.
Pricing for GPT-6 Luna (high) is $0.10 per 1M input tokens (competitively priced, median: $0.25) and $0.50 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $0.90). In total, it cost $47.64 to evaluate GPT-6 Luna (high) on the Intelligence Index.
